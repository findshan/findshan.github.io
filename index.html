I am a final-year undergraduate at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Liu Wei and Prof. Yin Jian. My long-term passion lies in foundational large language models — understanding how scale, structure, and optimization interact to produce emergent intelligence.

My current focus is agent harness engineering and systems performance — building reliable, low-latency runtimes for LLM-driven agents. Recent work spans GPU kernel optimization (4 hand-written Triton kernels, ~8× speedup on a fixed-model recommendation inference task — see the blog), open-source MCP runtime experimentation in Zig, and large-scale industrial recommendation systems — KDD Cup 2026 Top 5% nationally (rank 207 / 4000+) and TAAC2025 Top 4% as team leader.
