I am a final-year undergraduate at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Sun Yat-sen University, advised by Prof. Liu Wei and Prof. Yin Jian. My long-term goal is to contribute to AGI and ASI through foundational large language model research — understanding how scale, structure, and optimization interact to produce emergent intelligence.

My current focus spans foundation-model research, agent harness engineering, and systems performance — studying scalable LLM architectures while building reliable, low-latency runtimes for LLM-driven agents. Recent work spans GPU kernel optimization (four custom Triton kernels, ~8× speedup on a fixed-model recommendation inference task — see the blog), and large-scale industrial recommendation systems — KDD Cup 2026 Top 5% nationally (rank 207 / 4000+) and TAAC2025 Top 4% as team leader.
