SYSU Thesis 2026 SOHC: SO(n) Orthogonal Hyper-Connections via Cayley Transform
Wenshan Deng, advised by Prof. Liu Wei
Undergraduate Thesis · Sun Yat-sen University, 2026 In Progress

Introduces SO(n) rotation-group constraints into hyper-connection residual structures (mHC → SOHC) via Cayley transforms, building a dual-stream (isometric + semantic) orthogonal propagation mechanism. Uses Newton–Schulz iteration for convergence guarantees and 1/√N output scaling for gradient stability. Consistently outperforms mHC baselines on sequential recommendation: +8.5% MRR on ML-100K, +3.6% MRR on ML-1M.
