Engineering · GPU / Triton Baidu CTI: Generative Recommendation Inference Optimization
Wenshan Deng
Baidu Commercial AI Technology Innovation Competition, 2026

Optimized a fixed generative recommendation ranking model from approximately 238s to 30s (≈8×) without changing its architecture or sacrificing AUC/PCOC. Combined precision and attention-path restructuring with four hand-written Triton kernels for fused MoE, embedding pooling, and variable-length causal attention, each protected by numerical checks and dense fallbacks.

Competition · Multimodal Learning FinvCup: Multimodal Turn-Taking Prediction
Wenshan Deng
11th FinvCup FinTech Competition, 2026

Built a multimodal pipeline combining Whisper audio features, Qwen text representations, and historical turn-state signals for five-label event prediction. Reached 0.7437 in the preliminary round through fine-tuning, augmentation, checkpoint averaging, and per-label calibration; in the final round, diagnosed validation/private distribution shift and achieved the best private score of 0.609358 with calibrated thresholds rather than further overfitting the model.

ULTRA-HSTU-style Multi-task Recommendation on Alibaba Display Ads

Wenshan Deng
Independent Research Project, 2026

Adapted HSTU-style sequential modeling for click and post-click behavior prediction on Alibaba Display Ad data. Across four experiments from 100k to 26M samples, the full-scale run reached 0.6841 Click AUC and 0.9524 average behavior-task AUC.
